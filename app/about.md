## Our values

### Human first

Both on client work and internally, we value the people we are building for, and the people doing the building. Good work starts with genuine care.

### Close to the work

We believe you can’t solve a problem you don’t really understand. We stay near the work, ask questions others skip past, and resist the urge to abstract too early.

### Honest over comfortable

We say the difficult thing when it needs saying. That applies to how we challenge briefs, how we give feedback, and how we talk about what’s working and what isn’t.

### Making things real

We’re here to build things, and we measure ourselves by what gets built and used, not what gets presented and filed away.

### Curious by default

We start with questions, not answers. We ask why, follow the thread, and stay open to being wrong.
